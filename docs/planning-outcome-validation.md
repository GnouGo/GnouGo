# Planning outcome validation

Three authorities remain distinct: deterministic validation establishes technical facts, business Judges assess business fulfillment, and independent runtime oracles establish actual execution success. Compilation and favorable reports cannot authorize effects.

Generation is incremental. Each accepted construction change passes compiler preflight, while existing checkpoint journals can record pending business review. A technically valid producer is available work, not proof of fulfillment.

Review coherent business blocks spanning their fragments, dependencies, consumers and enclosing controls. Mechanical openings, unused group declarations and exact exports or closures do not independently trigger Judges. Review completed work before continuing beyond it; prefer finalization over a coincident local review. A small completed workflow can receive one global review and no local reviews. Every completed workflow requires a global business review before artifact approval.

The default projection contains applicable originals, actual arguments and values, executable transform instructions, meaningful producer semantics, controls, ordering, groups/calls, outputs, effects and cleanup. Global review includes all original obligations. Original contracts remain intact and compiler-enforced, with targeted retrieval for missing business-relevant detail. Receipts, hashes, tenant/session identities and dependency authority remain in host code, not a Judge manifest.

One schema-enforced verdict is issued:

- `ok`: current business behavior and completion claims are sound.
- `needs_change`: a precise violation cites original obligations and concrete implementation.
- `needs_context`: targeted originals are necessary before deciding.

Missing evidence is not a defect. Legitimate future work in open scopes is not a closure omission. Pointer validity establishes grounding, not semantic truth; no keyword or confidence filter adjudicates business conclusions. Unsupported or incomplete reports cannot accept work or create repair authority.

Context retrieval and the one supported reinterpretation of an unchanged review identity have separate bookkeeping. Every resulting call remains admitted and charged. Actual repairs use existing scoped correction authority and require deterministic revalidation and renewed review. A changed description alone cannot discharge an affected binding.

Artifact approval binds the exact completed workflow and mandatory global review. Benchmark delegated test review and individual invocation confirmations remain distinct from human review and production permissions. Independent oracles verify actual results, order, effects and cleanup after execution.
