# Planning

Construct the smallest sufficient business TaskPlan through bounded immediate decisions. Keep one authoritative plan and the existing compiler and execution engine. Production guidance is generic; scenario expectations belong in tests and independent benchmark oracles.

## Sources of truth

- Exact original requests, answers and MCP contracts establish requirements and permitted bindings. Navigation summaries never replace originals. Retain versions, hashes, permissions, effects and provenance in encrypted tenant-owned storage.
- Compiler preflight establishes technical facts about the present candidate: declarations, types, concrete references, availability, conditions and dependencies. An unfinished construction probe does not prove final validity or business fulfillment.
- Scoped Judges assess business meaning and the precise completion claims made by a fragment. Independent runtime oracles establish execution success. Compilation, a favorable review and policy eligibility never authorize execution.

## Progression and closure

Select the next necessary result from original obligations and accepted producers. Choose implementation after relevant capability discovery; supported local computation and structural work need no mandatory discovery call. Do not create preparatory copies, containers or aliases merely to subdivide the same action.

Retain each immediate step's original requirements, scope, expected result and exact prerequisites. A suspended consumer needs an explicit suspension decision, not an interpretation of its reselection prose. Keep a bounded continuation for the current prerequisite chain; derive available results from the accepted TaskPlan. Type similarity alone cannot identify the right producer. Reuse exact cached contracts and resume only after scope, contract and binding checks succeed. Continuations are non-executable metadata, not another scheduling graph.

Keep contribution, requirement closure, local export and public result distinct. Requirement associations do not claim completion. A nested export is not a public root output; foreach body exports become ordered arrays at the enclosing boundary. Preserve order, duplicates, exact identities, explicit nulls and complete observations.

Before general selection, inspect accepted closure facts. Prepare only a bounded structural closure whose obligations already have reviewed closure evidence and whose children, exports, choices, dependencies and cleanup are ready. Preserve existing bindings and run ordinary deterministic validation and scoped review. A childless scope alone does not establish completion. When only proven finalization remains, offer closure and evidence/clarification actions instead of more tasks. A reviewed closed requirement stays closed until explicit revision or dependency invalidation reopens it.

Host-prepared closures capture the same scoped candidate identity as fresh model drafts before validation or review; retained candidates and issued requests keep their captured comparison semantics.

Accepted fragments are immutable outside an authorized invalidation closure. Rollback reconstructs every invalidated task while preserving its effects and literals; it cannot withdraw accepted work. Present that restriction before choosing rollback and retain the original values in bounded rebuild context. Ordinary later tasks may consume prior same-scope results without exports; exports cross a container or reusable-group boundary. Open containers expose no consumable completion or outputs. Groups isolate declared inputs and are callable only when closed. Cleanup must retain its exact target and ownership after partial failure; success-only results cannot justify failure cleanup.

## Context and contracts

Use one instruction set per phase and only reachable semantic grammar. Keep exact source pages, original clauses, useful accepted interfaces and the current fragment bounded and retrievable. Resolve every candidate interface declaration explicitly; file content is not automatically a public return value. Preserve recursive type, enum, default and null semantics. Missing type evidence requires retrieval or clarification, never an invented `any`.

Drafts and scoped reviews retain bounded current-step companion-operation navigation with exact original retrieval addresses; navigation grants no executable contract or finding authority. Selected technical expectations are provisional implementation hints, not additional original obligations; preserve executable objectives and literals while resolving unsupported mechanisms against exact contracts.

The host owns deterministic provider pagination, cache lookup and mandatory schema closure. The model chooses semantic providers and operations. Preserve useful operation identities and exact description links after pages are consumed. Provider listing exhaustion does not make discovered operations unavailable. Never infer unavailable behavior from a partial description.

Compact schema views retain required siblings, coupled constraints, alternatives, referenced definitions, normative text, effects and permissions. Omitted presentation material has exact retrieval addresses. Admit complete follow-up requests, including expanded grammar, before accepting evidence batches. Repeated or regrouped evidence is not progress. An indivisible required closure that exceeds its captured ceiling stops explicitly; do not crop constraints or fill spare capacity with unrelated material.

Input targets guide presentation; captured ceilings bound the complete serialized request. Raising a necessary ceiling requires a newly frozen admitted profile, never mid-session escalation. Compact JSON guidance must preserve string contents and authoritative text.

## Review and correction

Validate a cloned candidate before review. Present exact compiler-derived consumed bindings, relevant caller declarations, accepted producers and export basis. Bounded accepted-operation context includes whole argument values, conditions and dependencies where they fit, with explicit omissions and exact retrieval addresses otherwise. An operation with no imported references still needs relevant prior-effect evidence; equal arguments alone do not prove unnecessary work. Selected imports are context hints for ordinary drafts, not a second permission system. `draft.outputs` lists additions; an empty list preserves accepted exports. Selected-scope state and remaining ancestor boundaries are distinct.

Require one explicit Judge action: completed findings, targeted evidence retrieval or inconclusive. Fresh structured findings separately identify an applicable original obligation and concrete implementation; their correctable location remains distinct. Pointer validity establishes grounding, not truth. Missing prompt evidence is not missing implementation. Current effects must satisfy applicable original constraints even while a scope is open; assess future obligations at their actual scope or requirement closure, not during a necessary opening or producer step.

Keep business findings advisory and contestable. Mechanically evaluate only existing deterministic diagnostics or explicitly structured factual assertions; never infer contradictions from keywords or treat a preflight summary as proof of business success. Finite loop bounds are safety guards: unspecified cardinality does not require unlimited execution. Reject excess input without truncated success; report conflicts only with authoritative required counts or bounds. Present this policy throughout fresh scoped review, including consumers and closures of accepted loops, not only when opening a loop.

Preserve each validated rejection's source, affected implementation, exact frozen-context identity and bounded before/after witness through its replacement chain, including candidate-free reselection and explicit consumer resumption. Acceptance ends that chain; unrelated later work must not inherit its findings. Do not reinterpret old JSON Pointers against a newer context. A changed artifact hash establishes novelty, not defect resolution. Recheck relevant deterministic bindings, declarations, exports and dependencies before another review. Reading a required input elsewhere or mentioning it in prose does not prove use by the required consumer.

Re-resolve necessary cited accepted-task evidence from the current plan only when its exact baseline, source, content and placement still match the retained evidence. Restoration is neither new evidence nor repair authority; stale or oversized support requires targeted retrieval.

Use the mandatory scoped review to assess semantic resolution, persistence or invalidation by evidence. Preserve executable objectives and literals, including transform instructions. Reject provably unchanged affected bindings; do not globally remove objectives from candidate identity. When a strategy fails, use an applicable bounded alternative—evidence, prerequisite, closure withdrawal or authorized invalidation—within existing correction allowances. Repeated non-progress stops.

Reuse the existing shared single follow-up allowance for supported reassessment or correction of a complete, bounded received report. Bind it to the same candidate and completion claim and persist its exact issuance. A report repair cannot edit the candidate, discard valid adverse findings or start another evidence cycle. Uncertain transport, output exhaustion and a second invalid report stop. Evidence and review calls consume calls, tokens, time and money, not TaskPlan corrections.

Coalesce local and intermediate reviews only when the issued bounded context explicitly covers both obligations, checkpoint joins and completion claims. Preserve that exact coverage through checkpoint acceptance and artifact approval; a combined review must satisfy both without inventing another receipt. Preserve trigger coverage and exact context identities; do not retrospectively reuse a narrower local review. There is no final whole-workflow Judge.

## Recovery, authorization and evaluation

Capture fresh behavior through the existing presentation version, keeping construction version 2 and storage format 10. Preserve historical future behavior, issued schemas, exact request bytes, receipts, hashes and approvals. Parse recovered responses according to their issued schema. Nullable source-generated metadata remains absent on historical records.

Persist transitions through existing checkpoints and journals. Never reconstruct a pending request from newer context, regenerate accepted fragments, reset cumulative accounting or replay an uncertain outcome. Dependency invalidation preserves independent hashes and original evidence.

Fresh synchronous tool-free planning permits at most three journaled HTTP attempts after completely received and durably recorded eligible transient responses. One owner reserves every physical attempt before dispatch against calls, tokens, time, financial limits and mandatory review headroom. Retain unknown-usage reservations even after a later success. Missing outcomes, interrupted bodies, invalid model responses, external effects and local fallbacks are not automatically retried. Saved successful receipts remain recoverable without a new dispatch. Exact authorized permanent abandonment retains all evidence and funds and forbids both replay and run continuation.

Execution requires artifact-bound approval and existing exact invocation permissions. Benchmark delegation stays isolated and is labeled `delegated_test_reviewed`, never human review. No favorable Judge can expand effects, workspace access or runtime inference authority.

Freeze source, settings, contracts, inputs and independent oracle versions before experiments. Preserve all failures. An empty or whitespace-heavy response does not establish its cause. Report estimated request components separately from provider-billed usage, logical requests separately from physical attempts, and technical passes separately from complete workflow execution. Count useful progress against independently authored business obligations, not aliases, tasks or checkpoints; no satisfied obligation means an undefined ratio.

Test observable behavior and historical byte compatibility, not incidental instruction wording. Keep concise current results and immutable evidence archives. No claimed convergence until complete workflows execute and satisfy their independent oracles.
