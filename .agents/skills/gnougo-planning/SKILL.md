# Planning

Build the smallest sufficient executable business TaskPlan. Favor reliable incremental work and clear contracts over additional planning abstractions or token-saving rules. A selected action produces its next needed result, even when its authoritative requirement spans the whole workflow. Offer exact retrieval addresses wherever evidence requests are available. Keep production guidance generic; scenario expectations belong in tests and independent benchmark oracles.

Keep three sources of truth distinct: compiler validation establishes technical facts; scoped Judges assess the business claims made by the current fragment; independent runtime oracles establish execution success. Reuse their existing evidence instead of adding another validator or planning abstraction.

## Core invariants

1. **Original intent is authoritative.** Retain exact requests and answer revisions in encrypted storage. Index coherent source pages with stable identities and explicit continuations. Extract every declared caller/public interface from its original clauses, combining only declarations of the same interface. Unrestricted strings and exact allowed-value sets remain distinct. Navigation summaries never replace original clauses. Unprocessed sources and unresolved requirements block completion.
2. **Construct one plan progressively.** Select the next business action, its original requirements and available inputs before fixing an implementation. Leave the task kind unresolved when it depends on capability discovery; bind it only after inspecting the relevant contracts. Supported local computation, closed reusable calls and structural work need no extra discovery call. Keep bounded prerequisite notes linked to original clauses or inspected contracts; available results come from the accepted TaskPlan, never a second scheduling graph. Build a demonstrated missing producer before its consumer. Keep the complete original requirement attached and open across its substeps. Branches, loops, reusable groups, cleanup and scope closure use the same coordinator. Accepted fragments are immutable outside an explicitly authorized invalidation closure; future obligations are metadata, never unresolved executable references.
3. **Original contracts remain executable authority.** Preserve MCP identities, versions, hashes, effects, permissions and full schemas. Descriptors and compact views aid selection. Retrieve exact branches with required ancestors, siblings, coupled constraints and referenced definitions intact. Show retrieval addresses for omitted material; never silently crop an indispensable constraint or infer a missing default.
4. **Separate technical validity, business review and execution evidence.** Apply candidates to a clone and reuse compiler preflight for present declarations, types, availability, contracts, provenance, conditions, dependencies and policy checks. Present its exact scoped facts to the Judge without implying that unfinished scopes, complete requirements or runtime effects were verified. Distinguish the selected scope's state from the candidate's remaining open boundaries; an open ancestor does not reopen its accepted children. Reuse compiler-derived interfaces within request construction; selected imports guide context without widening field access. Open containers expose no consumable completion or outputs; reusable groups remain isolated and callable only when closed. Final validation and execution authorization remain separate.
5. **Review only the current business claim.** Every valid candidate needs a local Judge. Coalesce intermediate triggers and explicitly review requirement closure against originals. Supply relevant declarations, actual typed operands and scope exports with their accepted or candidate basis. Include exact retained provider and method identities when present; identity alone does not prove runtime behavior. Missing prompt context is not missing implementation: retrieve exact evidence before alleging an omission. Findings must connect an applicable original obligation to concrete implementation evidence; a validated concrete location supplies that citation without repetition. Preserve the citation contract captured by issued schemas. Navigation and validation-status summaries alone are insufficient. Empty findings with residual execution uncertainty differ from an incomplete business review. Explicit inconclusive reports cannot accept a checkpoint or grant correction authority. There is no final whole-workflow Judge.
6. **Repair locally and require progress.** Use existing typed patches or bounded replacement for the current candidate. A tentative reselection states the precise obstacle and any missing prerequisite, preserving its scope and original requirements. Present both unresolved candidate findings and the latest rejected-action diagnostics; successful evidence retrieval clears only its resolved retrieval errors. Rebuild earlier work only through dependency-based invalidation, verifying unchanged independent fragments and commitments. Distinguish semantic candidate progress from new evidence. Under the fresh policy, a fully validated evidence-only response consumes calls, tokens and money, not a correction; actual correction/reselection attempts are charged once through durable receipts and checkpoints. Never refund historical charges or clear failure evidence to obtain another attempt. A valid inconclusive review may receive at most one reassessment for the same semantic candidate and completion claim, shared across local and intermediate review, only with materially better exact support. Persist its issuance with the exact request. Different wording or the same report is not progress; malformed reports, uncertain transport and exhausted allowances stop. Reassessment consumes calls and usage, not corrections, and cannot restart an evidence cycle.
7. **Capture budgets and recover exact work.** Resolve phase settings before dispatch through shared admission. Input targets guide presentation; hard ceilings bound complete serialized requests, including schemas and envelopes. Exceeding a target does not require another call. Reserve mandatory downstream reviews and preserve cumulative call, correction, token, time and financial limits. Persist exact requests and receipts; do not rebuild pending requests, regenerate accepted fragments, replay uncertainty or change captured settings during recovery.
8. **Execution requires separate authority.** Finalize only after complete requirement, scope, dependency, semantic, commitment, provenance and effect checks, then PlanningGraph validation and deterministic JavaScript → YAML v15 lowering. Compilation and Judges do not authorize effects or prove business success. Preserve artifact-bound approvals, tenant isolation, workspace confinement and exact invocation confirmations.

Request compact JSON formatting outside string values while preserving string contents and exact original text.

Use one clear instruction set per phase and only the selected task kind's shared semantic definitions. Select an executable business result; catalog discovery is a host phase, not a business result. Prefer one declaration shape when existing nullable DTO fields can express absence; preserve the distinction from explicit literal nulls and retain semantic validation. Show the bounded exact source clauses around the earliest unresolved requirement before selecting immediate work, so adjacent composition constraints inform its boundary. Retain meaningful bounded descriptions and call/operation identities for accepted work, with exact retrieval links. Drafts and Judges may navigate recent accepted siblings in their current scope alongside imported producers; inspect original task bindings before judging duplication. This context grants no edit authority and does not make intentionally incomplete scopes a failure. Issue only legal current choices from the bounded displayed directory; retrieve another page before selecting an omitted choice. Intent, selection and discovery need no TaskPlan grammar. Do not resend the full plan, history or capability catalog, or fill unused capacity with unrelated contracts.

Keep exact evidence navigation available throughout selection, reselection and drafting, including current scopes and closed reusable interfaces. A reselection that identifies a missing producer selects that prerequisite before returning to its consumer, within the same original requirements and scope. Each response chooses one action with all others null; commitments accompany an extension only, and evidence retrieval stages no commitments or other decision metadata. Evidence selects issued addresses, never prose requests or guessed field names. Prerequisite notes may cite an available input's original declaration in another requirement without claiming that requirement's completion or expanding edit authority. Share eligible original/contract citations between presentation and validation; accepted task/group navigation remains distinct from prerequisite authority. Couple operation identities to operation bindings and reusable targets to call bindings, matching the existing semantic rules instead of offering invalid field combinations. For a valid inconclusive review, the existing single reassessment may retrieve omitted accepted producers identified by verified consumed bindings and exact task evidence links. An objective alone does not show a computation; never infer retrieval targets from the Judge's prose or repeat already supplied support.

Interface classification is provisional until extraction resolves its exact sources. Determine whether a clause declares a caller input or a value returned to the caller before extracting its type. Writing an artifact and specifying its contents do not themselves declare a public result; an explicit returned value or path still does. Include bounded surrounding original clauses so names do not lose their earlier types or constraints. Extraction may explicitly identify a classified span as having no caller/public interface; its business obligation remains intact. Silent empty declarations cannot resolve coverage. Retrieve missing type evidence or clarify it instead of inventing `any`. Keep evidence, dispositions and typed declarations in the existing intent phase; narrow shared type grammar without introducing another type language. Keep essential null/default and enum semantics in the issued instructions when schema compaction removes annotations. Existing repair diagnostics should identify the rejected declaration's exact failing slot and bounded type/value facts without staging it or treating those facts as complete proof of nested constraints.

Keep a bounded, useful metadata directory and requested original evidence. Automatic operation descriptors follow only the current substep query; prior inspections remain exactly retrievable from retained originals, without being automatically included in later decisions. Present enough compact provider choices for a semantic decision without automatically inspecting their operations; preserve exact evidence pages and continuation addresses. Names locate candidates; original descriptions and necessary continuations establish their behavior. Inspected operations are a subset of the catalog: failure of those alternatives does not establish absence from uninspected providers. Retrieve the schemas needed for binding, and reselect a tentative step when its assumptions conflict with inspected contracts. Do not inspect later workflow actions or unrelated candidates once a suitable operation is established. Copy issued evidence addresses exactly, including hashes and offsets, and use already supplied evidence instead of fetching it again; a consumed navigation page does not invalidate its operation identity. Distinguish provider pagination from source health: exhausting a listing leaves discovered operations available. Keep provider cursors, evidence addresses and finding JSON Pointers distinct. Schema-optional inputs may still be required by the business action and must be retrieved before binding.

For fresh presentation, the host owns deterministic cursor navigation, cache lookup and mandatory schema closure. Follow only a selected provider and immediate query within the existing bounded read allowance, retaining completed results before advancing. Semantic provider and operation selection remains a model decision. Keep bounded operation identities and exact original-description links beside later evidence; consumed directory pages do not make an operation disappear. No automatic navigation may weaken contract constraints, repeat an uncertain call or select unrelated capabilities.

Expose the same bounded selected-scope owner and exact retrieval links during discovery, before binding the task kind. A necessary boundary and a useful producer are different decisions; show existing ownership rather than introducing container-count rules or another scheduling graph.

The fresh policy may retrieve coherent batches through the existing evidence envelope. Preserve requested originals in durable evidence and caches without making every earlier read mandatory prompt context. Avoid repeating metadata already bound into verified exact retrieval addresses; preserve full text, identities, effects, continuations and durable originals. Keep the current decision's necessary evidence visible, identify omitted cached material with exact retrieval links, and preserve complete mandatory constraint closures. Admit the complete prospective follow-up, including selected evidence, cumulative schema views and generation grammar, before accepting the batch. Clear stale candidate views when the candidate changes and expire obsolete discovery directories. Regrouping previously supplied evidence is not progress. Offer batches of unseen evidence separately from single description rereads under their existing captured knowledge guards. After an oversized discovery batch or multi-address review evidence batch, offer one exact evidence address at a time until that admission diagnosis resolves. Review evidence rejection grants no correction authority; an oversized singleton stops with its exact admission diagnosis, without accepting partial evidence. This changes presentation, not the retained allowance or original constraints. Partial descriptions do not prove missing functionality; follow necessary original continuations before that conclusion. An indivisible mandatory closure that exceeds admission still stops explicitly.

Share necessary task-kind semantics without mixing phase actions. Judges need the meaning of bindings, collections and closures; only authors receive instructions to reselect, edit exports or expand generation grammar. Deterministic bindings assemble and compute declared values; transforms extract observed facts or interpret supplied inputs. Interpretation passes the task objective as the runtime LLM instruction and resultType as its output contract; it needs no separate expression and never simulates MCP effects. Clarify material missing business facts through the existing interaction loop; caller-input bindings do not require concrete runtime values during planning.

## Composition and correction boundaries

- Preserve exact content, opaque identities, order, duplicates and explicit nulls. Missing differs from null; defaults require authoritative declarations. Complete observations cannot be silently sampled or truncated.
- Data references imply dependencies. Scope exports and legal ancestor captures preserve availability; conditional branches expose compatible interfaces. Keep normal results in the normal path and genuine cleanup in finalizers.
- `requires` is a mandatory Boolean assertion; `conditional` routes alternatives. Preserve short-circuit order, nullability and existing runtime limits. Loop bounds are safety guards: unspecified input length does not require unlimited capacity. Preserve every item within admitted capacity and reject oversized inputs without truncated success. Report conflicts with explicit required capacities or known required item counts; keep unspecified capacity unverified. A presentation default alone does not establish a business restriction.
- The model authors semantic tasks and bindings. The compiler derives technical dependencies, typed projections and v15 executor mappings. Neither compilation nor correction may invent business decisions, external effects or producer ownership.
- Stage tentative operation/result commitments with candidates and accept them atomically with checkpoints. Requirement IDs and operation commitments annotate contributed work; alone they do not establish progress or close a requirement. Reviews assess full obligations only for the exact issued requirement closures, scope closures or plan completion. Preserve accepted commitments unless an authorized revision invalidates them.

Read-only context grants no edit authority. Append ordinary sequential work to an appropriate existing open scope. A new container must add a needed control, reuse, cleanup or export boundary, not merely subdivide or defer the same business action. Select a necessary opening, child implementation and closure as separate immediate actions: opening establishes the boundary and control inputs, not the future body's result or completion of its wider business requirement. Keep one bounded selected-owner description and its exact task link alongside local task navigation, so authors and Judges can compare the new boundary with its accepted context. Do not export an open container's unavailable result. Assess exports against the combined accepted scope and candidate: root-scope exports are public results, nested exports remain local, and an empty draft export list preserves accepted declarations. Keep corrections inside the candidate or proven invalidation region, preserving unrelated arguments, effects, interfaces, cleanup and accepted decisions. When an existing rollback action is offered, explain that it requests host-computed dependency invalidation, not direct edits to accepted work. Stop exhausted allowances, repeated non-progress, uncertain completion or unprovable preservation.

## Compatibility, evaluation and evidence

Fresh presentation revision 4 grounds business reviews in existing compiler facts and adds an explicit inconclusive action to the private review envelope. Keep construction version 2, storage format 10 and public interfaces unchanged. Preserve revision-3 future requests and historical deserialization defaults, issued schemas, receipts, approvals, hashes and compiler profiles exactly. Gate new behavior through captured fresh settings; do not add a protocol flag for each prompt adjustment. Retain compatibility paths needed for continuation and artifacts, without making them selectable for new monolithic generation.

Test observable behavior and necessary invariants. Preserve exact-byte tests for historical requests; avoid brittle assertions on incidental prompt wording and redundant fixture copies. Verify emitted requests, full constraint closures, interruption recovery, accounting and independently executed business outcomes.

Evaluate reasoning, output reservations and presentation as controlled experiments with frozen source, settings, contracts, inputs and oracle versions. An empty reasoning-only response does not establish its cause. Use fresh identities, retain failures, and distinguish estimates from billed provider usage. Separate pre-dispatch admission failures from dispatched calls with unknown outcomes. Independently grounded static findings establish candidate defects, not observed runtime failures. A smaller prompt, accepted checkpoint or green CI is not business acceptance.

Evaluate extraction-guidance simplification separately from schema changes. Preserve original source dispositions, type/default/null/enum semantics and historical request bytes; record exact emitted context and schema equality before attributing differences to instructions.

Measure useful progress against independently frozen business obligations, not task count, aliases, containers, model-created requirement splits or checkpoints. Report calls per satisfied obligation alongside absolute usage, latency, cost and actual runtime acceptance; an empty denominator is unavailable, not success. New presentation behavior uses one captured revision of the existing setting. Historical sessions and issued requests retain their exact behavior and identities.

Paid calls and effects require the user's existing authorization. An isolated host may narrow discovery to the effect authority it supports, reusing its execution allowlist and preserving each original contract; discoverability never authorizes arguments or execution. Benchmark delegation remains isolated, artifact-bound and labeled truthfully; only exact individual runtime confirmations may authorize permitted effects. A permanently retained inconclusive call keeps its full unknown-usage reservation and original evidence, and forbids both replay and continuation of its abandoned run. An interrupted process with no recorded outcome remains uncertain; never manufacture a failure or completion to make it recoverable. Restored provider availability does not establish the earlier call's outcome.

Fresh sessions may capture at most three journaled HTTP attempts for synchronous, tool-free planning after fully received and persisted transient server responses or positively identified temporary rate limits. Reuse the existing transport helper; do not retry missing outcomes, interrupted bodies, invalid model responses, external effects or local fallbacks. One owner admits and accounts for each physical attempt against cumulative calls, tokens, money, time and remaining mandatory reviews before dispatch. Failed attempts without verified usage retain their full reservation even after a later success. Exact requests, backoff and outcomes govern recovery; historical policies remain unchanged. Exhaustion stops without automatic abandonment. Capture planning deadlines separately from execution deadlines, and raise a necessary request ceiling only in a newly frozen profile admitted by the model and remaining budget.

Keep maintained documentation concise and preserve immutable audit evidence through explicit archive links.
