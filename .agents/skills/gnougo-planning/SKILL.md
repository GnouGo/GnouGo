# Planning

Construct the smallest sufficient business TaskPlan through bounded decisions, using one authoritative plan, compiler and execution engine. Keep production rules generic and scenario expectations in tests/oracles.

## Authority

Exact original requests, answers and MCP contracts establish obligations and permitted bindings. Navigation and model-authored expectations add no authority. Preserve exact identities, versions, hashes, effects, permissions and provenance in encrypted tenant-owned storage.

Compiler preflight proves present technical facts, not business fulfillment or final validity. Mandatory scoped Judges assess meaning and explicit completion claims; findings remain advisory, and grounding does not prove truth. Runtime oracles establish execution success. Execution still requires artifact-bound approval and exact permissions, with tenant/workspace isolation and cleanup ownership. Isolated benchmark delegation is `delegated_test_reviewed`, never human review or expanded inference/effect authority.

## Construction

Derive the next decision from original obligations, accepted checkpoints, accessible producers, suspended consumers and legal closures. Keep this view request-local: neither task presence nor technical validity proves fulfillment. Reuse an exact accepted binding before creating work; a demonstrated defective producer needs authorized revision, not a compensating copy. Operation names and type similarity establish neither redundancy nor semantic suitability.

Record prerequisite-to-consumer bindings with the producer draft and its scoped review. Promote them only with the accepted checkpoint. Fresh presentation version 8 may preserve an association across independently reviewed, demonstrably disjoint accepted additions using prospectively captured dependency evidence in the existing review journal. Cover transitive inputs, groups, captures, exports, control/order, originals, claims, permissions and complete contracts; unchanged producer bytes or absent graph edges alone prove no independence. Missing evidence and unknown interactions retain semantic selection. Historical records retain their original guards. Changes to associations change review claims even if executable bytes are unchanged.

Before semantic selection, apply an established resumption, exact cached operation, eligible evidence continuation or proven closure through the existing validators and persisted transitions. Record its reason and exact evidence, or the decision that remains unresolved, without another model call or progress store. A proven host closure retains every original requirement even beyond the model selection's presentation bound; ordinary admission and scoped review still apply.

Use the existing selection and validation paths for inspected, authorized operation identities; unknown capabilities still require discovery. Follow explicitly requested original-description continuation links mechanically within the existing evidence batch and complete-request limits. Selected requirement-only closure and scope closure are distinct claims. Prepare an unchanged-plan closure directly only when the explicit choice needs no new work or bindings; it still requires ordinary validation and business review. Keep the stronger reviewed-evidence conditions for closure prepared before selection.

Keep contribution, requirement closure, local export and public return distinct. Ordinary partial closure cannot claim a requirement whose complete current direct source disposition declares a required public output absent from the merged root. Optional outputs and supporting evidence create no such obligation; authorized revisions retain partial-repair behavior. This necessary check does not prove fulfillment. Host-prepared closure still needs the same scoped identity, reviewed evidence and ordinary validation; an empty scope is not completion.

Name presentation completeness as text coverage, never business completion. Closure diagnostics identify the boundaries actually still open so corrections preserve completed children and address the rejected claim.

Accepted work is immutable outside explicit revision or authorized dependency invalidation. Rebuild original effects, literals, scope and ordering; rollback cannot withdraw work. Reviewed requirements stay closed until legitimately reopened. Respect open-container availability, isolated group inputs and export boundaries. Preserve identities, ordered foreach arrays, duplicates, nulls and complete observations. Cleanup targets must survive failure.

Resolve current candidate interface sources explicitly. Supporting originals and accepted names provide context, not extra candidates. Fill unused bounded interface-context slots with immediate original-page neighbors after existing priorities. Keep source coverage, existing declarations and recursive type/default/enum/null semantics intact; distinguish missing, unreferenced and duplicate typed names. Artifact contents are not automatically public outputs. Missing evidence requires retrieval or clarification, never invented `any`.

Offer reachable grammar using the complete accepted symbol inventory: no tasks means no producer references. Selection input names come from complete declarations for the offered scopes; isolated group parameters remain distinct from caller inputs. Tentative commitments pair each operation only with its declared ports. An operation objective describes contribution without enlarging its contract; transform objectives remain executable. Extract observed facts or interpret supplied data into typed results through the configured runtime LLM, without inventing external observations or effects.

Omitted producer input constraints and defaults remain retrievable through the same original-contract evidence; retrieval neither applies defaults nor selects an operation. Preserve their compiler-resolved nullable interfaces, full original operation and top-level port descriptions, accepted producer arguments and bound-input constraints; explicitly retrieved originals stay intact. ## Review and correction

Require applicable local and intermediate reviews. Keep fresh Judge instructions in one coherent block; preserve historical guidance and issued requests. A Judge returns findings, targeted evidence requests or explicit inconclusive status. Findings separately identify an applicable original obligation, concrete implementation and correctable location. Present arguments/effects must satisfy original constraints even in open scopes; assess future work at its actual closure. A contract prerequisite proves neither availability nor permission expansion. Missing evidence remains unresolved.

Present exact original clauses independently of selected associations and completion claims. When originals exceed one request, review the same fragment against bounded whole-clause windows, preserving linked continuations. Existing review records bind coverage to the candidate, baseline, claims, originals and contracts. Every required window must pass before acceptance, automatic closure or approval; a clean window closes no requirement. Assess the current window against unchanged full candidate claims; another window's original is needed here only for a specific dependency or coupled constraint of a current clause. Keep only that window's originals in its context and retain exact retrieval links. An indivisible required context must fit its captured ceiling. Reserve remaining reviews and share the single follow-up allowance across windows. Financial preflight accounts for every remaining call at its captured ceiling, without a fixed phase-count cap. Fresh evidence schemas enumerate host-issued addresses, never arbitrary prose or schema content.

Reuse applicable producer facts only with their original checkpoint/review provenance and verified dependency evidence. They are not new clean windows: new fragments, interactions and completion claims retain full original coverage, including global and negative constraints. Compact only identical material within a request, retaining complete schemas, original coordinates and exact retrieval; a previous request is not model memory. Measure complete serialized requests and actual calls separately rather than inferring savings from fewer displayed fields.

Mechanically enforce existing deterministic diagnostics or explicit factual assertions, not keyword interpretations, semantic duplicate bans or preflight-as-business-proof. Finite loop ceilings reject excess input rather than sample success; only authoritative required bounds establish a conflict.

Carry validated rejection identities and bounded before/after evidence through the same replacement chain, including reselection and explicit resumption; acceptance ends the chain. Never reinterpret old pointers against new contexts. Restore cited accepted evidence only when baseline, source, content and placement still match; restoration is not progress or authority. Historical technical rejections are not current diagnostics or edit slots. Validation summaries must describe the current candidate truthfully.

A changed hash proves novelty, not resolution. Preserve executable objectives and literals, reject provably unchanged affected bindings and require scoped review after correction. Mentioning an input elsewhere does not establish required consumer use. Use offered evidence, prerequisites, claim reselection or authorized invalidation within existing allowances; repeated non-progress stops.

For independently established defects in accepted work, an authorized host uses the existing explicit revision command with current identity and exact editable paths. Technical revalidation cannot discharge its pending repair obligation: preserve that exact host diagnostic while the accepted candidate is unchanged, then require an authorized change and renewed scoped review. Keep host intent distinct from compiler findings; never reconstruct lost authority or alter issued requests. Preserve requirements and independent checkpoints; inspect dependent bindings, retain failed snapshots and renew review and artifact approval. Rollback cannot change literals it promises to preserve, and compensating runtime effects cannot repair a planning node. Benchmark delegation does not expand production revision or execution authority.

Keep the existing shared single follow-up allowance for supported reassessment or repair of a complete bounded received report. Persist its exact candidate/claim identity. It cannot edit the candidate, erase valid adverse findings or restart evidence cycles. Uncertain transport, output exhaustion and a second invalid report stop. Evidence/review consume usage, not TaskPlan corrections. Coalesce reviews only with issued coverage of both obligations, joins and claims, preserved through checkpoints and approval. Never invent receipts or reuse narrower coverage. There is no final whole-workflow Judge.

## Recovery and evaluation

Capture fresh behavior through the existing presentation version; retain construction version 2 and storage format 10. Preserve historical future behavior and exact issued requests, schemas, receipts, hashes and approvals. Parse saved responses under their issued schema. Never rebuild pending requests from newer context, regenerate accepted work, reset accounting or replay uncertainty; use existing checkpoints/journals. A legacy benchmark retention exception requires independently pinned pre-dispatch source/build/input proof and the exact original dispatch mapping; a mode label never establishes eligibility or authorization. Bind legacy run identities to their exact issuer and pinned invocation: corpus keys need not contain cohort metadata, and a live scenario identifier is distinct from its display name and explicit run label.

One owner reserves every physical attempt before dispatch against calls, tokens, time, money and review headroom. Fresh synchronous tool-free planning permits at most three journaled attempts after fully received, durably recorded eligible transient failures. Missing outcomes, interrupted bodies, invalid responses and external effects are not automatically retried. Keep unknown-usage reserves even after success; saved successes recover without dispatch. Exact authorized abandonment retains evidence and funds and forbids replay and run continuation. Fresh benchmark runtime inference is single-attempt with prior reservation, retaining unverified failures including rate limits. Preserve historical policies; do not infer new retention eligibility.

Freeze source/settings/contracts/inputs/oracles before experiments. Validate an implemented correction before adding another mechanism. Separate instruction experiments from schema/settings changes and retain failures. Missing receipts, received output exhaustion, formatting and semantic mistakes are distinct evidence; none establishes another's cause. Separate estimates/billed usage, logical/physical calls and technical/execution results. Useful progress uses independent business obligations; none satisfied means an undefined ratio. Test behavior and historical bytes, not wording. Claim convergence only after complete execution passes independent oracles.

Use explicit temporary workspace roots in tests, including contract-only policy capture; unrelated assertions must not access the developer's default workspace.
