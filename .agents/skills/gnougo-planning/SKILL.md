# Planning

Build the smallest sufficient executable business TaskPlan. Favor reliable incremental work and clear contracts over additional planning abstractions or token-saving rules. A selected action produces its next needed result, even when its authoritative requirement spans the whole workflow. Offer exact retrieval addresses wherever evidence requests are available. Keep production guidance generic; scenario expectations belong in tests and independent benchmark oracles.

## Core invariants

1. **Original intent is authoritative.** Retain exact requests and answer revisions in encrypted storage. Index coherent source pages with stable identities and explicit continuations. Extract every declared caller/public interface from its original clauses, combining only declarations of the same interface. Unrestricted strings and exact allowed-value sets remain distinct. Navigation summaries never replace original clauses. Unprocessed sources and unresolved requirements block completion.
2. **Construct one plan progressively.** Select the next business action, its original requirements and available inputs before fixing an implementation. Leave the task kind unresolved when it depends on capability discovery; bind it only after inspecting the relevant contracts. Supported local computation, closed reusable calls and structural work need no extra discovery call. Keep bounded prerequisite notes linked to original clauses or inspected contracts; available results come from the accepted TaskPlan, never a second scheduling graph. Build a demonstrated missing producer before its consumer. Keep the complete original requirement attached and open across its substeps. Branches, loops, reusable groups, cleanup and scope closure use the same coordinator. Accepted fragments are immutable outside an explicitly authorized invalidation closure; future obligations are metadata, never unresolved executable references.
3. **Original contracts remain executable authority.** Preserve MCP identities, versions, hashes, effects, permissions and full schemas. Descriptors and compact views aid selection. Retrieve exact branches with required ancestors, siblings, coupled constraints and referenced definitions intact. Show retrieval addresses for omitted material; never silently crop an indispensable constraint or infer a missing default.
4. **Validate semantics before review or acceptance.** Apply candidates to a clone and reuse compiler preflight for types, availability, contracts, provenance, conditions, dependencies and permissions. Open containers expose no consumable completion or outputs; reusable groups are isolated and callable only when closed. For the fresh simplified policy, selected imports guide context; preflight determines the exact accessible references actually used, without widening a selected field to its whole producer. Explicit revision authority remains binding.
5. **Review only the current claim.** Every valid candidate needs a local Judge. Coalesce existing intermediate-review triggers and explicitly review requirement closure against original evidence. An open scope is not a completion claim; a needed producer may precede its consumer or public export. Cleanup may follow creation when its exact target and ownership remain available after partial failure; required creation-time capture cannot be deferred. Verify cleanup at closure. Distinguish public root outputs from local exports and declared caller inputs from imported operands. Supply actual typed imports and closure interfaces; unchanged unused accepted exports are navigable exact evidence when an ordinary candidate adds no exports and makes no scope, requirement or plan closure claim. Keep grounded object-level reference choices. Invalid reports cannot accept checkpoints; uncertainty remains unverified. There is no final whole-workflow Judge.
6. **Repair locally and require progress.** Use existing typed patches or bounded replacement for the current candidate. A tentative reselection states the precise obstacle and any missing prerequisite, preserving its scope and original requirements. Present both unresolved candidate findings and the latest rejected-action diagnostics; successful evidence retrieval clears only its resolved retrieval errors. Rebuild earlier work only through dependency-based invalidation, verifying unchanged independent fragments and commitments. Distinguish semantic candidate progress from new evidence. Under the fresh policy, a fully validated evidence-only response consumes calls, tokens and money, not a correction; actual correction/reselection attempts are charged once through durable receipts and checkpoints. Never refund historical charges or clear failure evidence to obtain another attempt.
7. **Capture budgets and recover exact work.** Resolve phase settings before dispatch through shared admission. Input targets guide presentation; hard ceilings bound complete serialized requests, including schemas and envelopes. Exceeding a target does not require another call. Reserve mandatory downstream reviews and preserve cumulative call, correction, token, time and financial limits. Persist exact requests and receipts; do not rebuild pending requests, regenerate accepted fragments, replay uncertainty or change captured settings during recovery.
8. **Execution requires separate authority.** Finalize only after complete requirement, scope, dependency, semantic, commitment, provenance and effect checks, then PlanningGraph validation and deterministic JavaScript → YAML v15 lowering. Compilation and Judges do not authorize effects or prove business success. Preserve artifact-bound approvals, tenant isolation, workspace confinement and exact invocation confirmations.

## Presentation and discovery

Use one clear instruction set per phase and only the selected task kind's shared semantic definitions. Prefer one declaration shape when existing nullable DTO fields can express absence; preserve the distinction from explicit literal nulls and retain semantic validation. Show the bounded exact source clauses around the earliest unresolved requirement before selecting immediate work, so adjacent composition constraints inform its boundary. Retain meaningful bounded descriptions and call/operation identities for accepted work, with exact retrieval links. Drafts and Judges may navigate recent accepted siblings in their current scope alongside imported producers; inspect original task bindings before judging duplication. This context grants no edit authority and does not make intentionally incomplete scopes a failure. Issue only legal current choices from the bounded displayed directory; retrieve another page before selecting an omitted choice. Intent, selection and discovery need no TaskPlan grammar. Do not resend the full plan, history or capability catalog, or fill unused capacity with unrelated contracts.

Keep exact evidence navigation available when moving from selection into drafting, including current scopes and closed reusable interfaces. Evidence responses select issued addresses, never prose requests or guessed field names. Prerequisite notes may cite an available input's original declaration in another requirement without claiming that requirement's completion or expanding edit authority. Share eligible original/contract citations between presentation and validation; accepted task/group navigation remains distinct from prerequisite authority. Couple call-only fields to the call shape so structural openings cannot accidentally bind a reusable target.

Interface classification is provisional until extraction resolves its exact sources. Include bounded surrounding original clauses so names do not lose their earlier types or constraints. Extraction may explicitly identify a classified span as having no caller/public interface; its business obligation remains intact. Silent empty declarations cannot resolve coverage. Retrieve missing type evidence or clarify it instead of inventing `any`. Keep evidence, dispositions and typed declarations in the existing intent phase; narrow shared type grammar without introducing another type language. Keep essential null/default and enum semantics in the issued instructions when schema compaction removes annotations.

Keep a bounded, useful metadata directory and requested original evidence. Names locate candidates; original descriptions and necessary continuations establish their behavior. An uninspected candidate is not evidence that a capability is unavailable. Retrieve the schemas needed for binding, and reselect a tentative step when its assumptions conflict with inspected contracts. Do not inspect later workflow actions or unrelated candidates once a suitable operation is established. Copy issued evidence addresses exactly, including hashes and offsets, and use already supplied evidence instead of fetching it again; a consumed navigation page does not invalidate its operation identity. Distinguish provider pagination from source health: exhausting a listing leaves discovered operations available. Keep provider cursors, evidence addresses and finding JSON Pointers distinct. Schema-optional inputs may still be required by the business action and must be retrieved before binding.

For fresh presentation, the host owns deterministic cursor navigation, cache lookup and mandatory schema closure. Follow only a selected provider and immediate query within the existing bounded read allowance, retaining completed results before advancing. Semantic provider and operation selection remains a model decision. Keep bounded operation identities and exact original-description links beside later evidence; consumed directory pages do not make an operation disappear. No automatic navigation may weaken contract constraints, repeat an uncertain call or select unrelated capabilities.

The fresh policy may retrieve coherent batches through the existing evidence envelope. Keep explicitly requested originals available within the current decision; fetching another page or provider must not silently discard them. Admit the complete prospective follow-up, including retained evidence, cumulative schema views and generation grammar, before accepting the batch. Clear stale candidate views when the candidate changes and expire obsolete discovery directories. Regrouping previously supplied evidence is not progress. Description rereads retain their captured knowledge guards. An oversized separable request can use smaller exact pages; an indivisible mandatory closure that exceeds admission stops explicitly.

Share necessary task-kind semantics between selection and drafting rather than adding a prompt rule for every failure. Deterministic bindings assemble and compute declared values; transforms extract observed facts or interpret supplied inputs. Interpretation passes the task objective as the runtime LLM instruction and resultType as its output contract; it needs no separate expression and never simulates MCP effects. Clarify material missing business facts through the existing interaction loop; caller-input bindings do not require concrete runtime values during planning.

## Composition and correction boundaries

- Preserve exact content, opaque identities, order, duplicates and explicit nulls. Missing differs from null; defaults require authoritative declarations. Complete observations cannot be silently sampled or truncated.
- Data references imply dependencies. Scope exports and legal ancestor captures preserve availability; conditional branches expose compatible interfaces. Keep normal results in the normal path and genuine cleanup in finalizers.
- `requires` is a mandatory Boolean assertion; `conditional` routes alternatives. Preserve short-circuit order, nullability and existing runtime limits. Loop bounds are safety guards; assess explicit original capacity requirements and retain unspecified capacity as unverified. A presentation default alone does not establish a business restriction.
- The model authors semantic tasks and bindings. The compiler derives technical dependencies, typed projections and v15 executor mappings. Neither compilation nor correction may invent business decisions, external effects or producer ownership.
- Stage tentative operation/result commitments with candidates and accept them atomically with checkpoints. A contribution does not close a requirement. Preserve accepted commitments unless an authorized revision invalidates them.

Read-only context grants no edit authority. Keep corrections inside the candidate or proven invalidation region, preserving unrelated arguments, effects, interfaces, cleanup and accepted decisions. When an existing rollback action is offered, explain that it requests host-computed dependency invalidation, not direct edits to accepted work. Stop exhausted allowances, repeated non-progress, uncertain completion or unprovable preservation.

## Compatibility, evaluation and evidence

Keep historical deserialization defaults, issued schemas, receipts, approvals, hashes and compiler profiles exact. Gate new behavior through captured fresh settings; do not add a protocol flag for each prompt adjustment. Retain compatibility paths needed for continuation and artifacts, without making them selectable for new monolithic generation.

Test observable behavior and necessary invariants. Preserve exact-byte tests for historical requests; avoid brittle assertions on incidental prompt wording and redundant fixture copies. Verify emitted requests, full constraint closures, interruption recovery, accounting and independently executed business outcomes.

Evaluate reasoning, output reservations and presentation as controlled experiments with frozen source, settings, contracts, inputs and oracle versions. An empty reasoning-only response does not establish its cause. Use fresh identities, retain failures, and distinguish estimates from billed provider usage. A smaller prompt, accepted checkpoint or green CI is not business acceptance.

Measure useful progress against independently frozen business obligations, not task count, aliases, containers, model-created requirement splits or checkpoints. Report calls per satisfied obligation alongside absolute usage, latency, cost and actual runtime acceptance; an empty denominator is unavailable, not success. New presentation behavior uses one captured revision of the existing setting. Historical sessions and issued requests retain their exact behavior and identities.

Paid calls and effects require the user's existing authorization. Benchmark delegation remains isolated, artifact-bound and labeled truthfully; only exact individual runtime confirmations may authorize permitted effects. A permanently retained inconclusive call keeps its full unknown-usage reservation and original evidence, and forbids both replay and continuation of its abandoned run. An interrupted process with no recorded outcome remains uncertain; never manufacture a failure or completion to make it recoverable. Restored provider availability does not establish the earlier call's outcome.

Keep maintained documentation concise and preserve immutable audit evidence through explicit archive links.
